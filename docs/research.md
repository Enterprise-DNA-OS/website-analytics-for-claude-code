## Primary evidence

- [Matomo pricing](https://matomo.org/pricing/): structured USD 1,900 monthly Cloud offer at ten million hits, and free self-hosted Community. The visible page says higher Cloud allowances require contact, so no larger structured tier is quoted.
- [Matomo raw visit export](https://matomo.org/faq/how-to/faq_24536/): Live.getLastVisitsDetails, JSON, pagination and action-detail limits.
- [Reporting API](https://developer.matomo.org/guides/reporting-api): supported output formats and parameters.
- [Report export formats](https://matomo.org/faq/how-to/faq_26727/): distinguishes summary reports from raw visit history.
- [Matomo public demo](https://demo.matomo.cloud/?module=API&method=Live.getLastVisitsDetails&idSite=1&period=day&date=yesterday&format=JSON&filter_limit=1): inspected for native field names; committed examples are fictional.
- [Orderful transaction export](https://docs.orderful.com/changelog/download-purchase-orders-from-the-transaction-list-as-csv): shortlisted import route, rejected before build.
- [SiliconExpert FAQ](https://www.siliconexpert.com/faqs/): custom quotes and licensed component data.
- [Privacy principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) and [principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/): source rules for the operational control notes.

The ten questions demonstrate shipped queries, not a claim that Matomo lacks equivalent reports. Its own reporting and customisation capabilities are broader than this base. The value proposition is a smaller owned review workflow with business-specific follow-up and an optional managed service.
